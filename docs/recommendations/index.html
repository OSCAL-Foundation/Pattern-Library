Recommendations

What the Foundation recommends, and the analysis each recommendation came out of.

A recommendation lives here rather than inside the analysis that produced it. It is what a reader will cite, and it has a lifecycle of its own: proposed, then accepted, then superseded by a later one. The analysis it came from is named on it, and that analysis names it back.

None published yet. One analysis is open and it has not reached a recommendation.

The open effort is a comparison that states three approaches and prices what each one costs, and it says on every page that it makes no recommendation. An area with nothing in it is the honest rendering of that, so this page says so rather than standing empty.

When an effort does reach one, it becomes an area under docs/recommendations/ with a recommendation.json naming the analysis it came from, an entry appended to recommendations.json, and the recommendation field filled in on the analysis it settles.
