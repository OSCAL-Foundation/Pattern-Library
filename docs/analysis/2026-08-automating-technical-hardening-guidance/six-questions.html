The six questions considered for every approach

Whatever model holds a benchmark recommendation, six questions have to be answered. These questions factor capturing the rule, the bridge to execute a runtime (check) and how flexible answers can be scoped and provided.

1. The six questions, one at a time

Two example rules are used across each option. One is protection of data at rest, a condition that is either met or not. The other is a minimum password length, the same shape but carrying a value, which is what makes it the rule that shows where each approach puts a parameter.

Every question below is then put to all three approaches using those same two rules.

A worked scenario puts one real system to all three and counts what each would produce. Each approach then has a page of its own, carrying its strengths and risks: Catalog-first, Component-first and Assessment-first. Open questions carries what is unresolved.

This site makes no recommendation. It states three approaches in the terms their proponents use and shows the content each one ships.
