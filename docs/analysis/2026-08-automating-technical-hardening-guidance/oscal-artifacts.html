What each approach has published, one row per document, with what is in it counted from the file.

The inventory loads from data/oscal-artifacts.json.

The shapes are the argument in miniature. One group publishes a catalog and a component definition per service, one publishes component definitions carrying rules with a plan and a result beside them, and one publishes assessment plans and nothing else. Each is internally consistent, and no two of them meet.

The six questions puts the same two rules through all three shapes, one question at a time.

This site makes no recommendation. It states three approaches in the terms their proponents use and shows the content each one ships.
