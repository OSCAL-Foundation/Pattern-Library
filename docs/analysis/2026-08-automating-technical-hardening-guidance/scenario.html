One system, three hardening guides

A system under NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 at the High baseline, running Ubuntu, PostgreSQL and Microsoft 365, hardened against a published guide for each. The same scenario is modelled in all three approaches, using every OSCAL model except the plan of action and milestones.

1. The system, and what it is held to

2.1 Catalog-first, 18 files

Each guide is published as a catalog, selected by a profile of its own, and tied back to the framework by a mapping collection. The plan of record answers every control in the merged set.

2.2 Component-first, 11 files

Each guide becomes rules on the component it configures, and each engine that tests them becomes a component of its own. The framework side is unchanged.

2.3 Assessment-first, 11 files

Each guide becomes an assessment plan. The framework side is unchanged, and each plan produces a result of its own.

This site makes no recommendation. It states three approaches in the terms their proponents use and shows the content each one ships.
